Reminder: logit effective distance

We have the logit route choice probability as

\[ \pi_r = \frac{e^{-\eta c_r}}{Z_{ij}}, \qquad Z_{ij} = \sum_{s \in \mathcal{R}_{ij}} e^{-\eta c_s}, \qquad p^a_{ij} = \sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}_{ij}} \pi_r\,[\![a \in r]\!] \]

Then define the logit route effective distance as

\[ d_{ij} = -\frac{1}{\eta}\ln \sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}_{ij}} e^{-\eta c_r} \]

where for sufficiently large \(\eta\), \(d_{ij} \approx\) SPT effective distance.

Note: The Prior-Constraints Seesaw

  • the new formulation of OD pair effective distance conveys information about alternative paths to the gravity model
  • different regularization settings reveal this:
    • if regularization parameter \(\sigma\) goes up (less trust in edge flow data), then
    • logit routing dispersion parameter \(\eta\) goes down (allow more alternate paths)
    • gravity penalty parameter \(\beta\) goes up (more trust in logit effective distance)
    • inference quality stays very similar after calibration, even though constraints are used less in optimization

Reminder: intervention analysis

If I act to change the flow of edge a’, how does this impact \(T_{ij}\)?

\[ \theta = (\lambda_1, \lambda_2, ... , \alpha_1, \alpha_2, ..., \beta_1, \beta_2, ...) \]

\[ m_{ij} = \big(p^1_{ij}, \dots, p^E_{ij},\; \underbrace{0,\dots,1,\dots,0}_{i},\; \underbrace{0,\dots,1,\dots,0}_{j}\big) \]

So: \(\log T_{ij}^* = \log T_{ij}^0 - m_{ij}^T \theta\). If \(V_a \rightarrow V_a + \delta V_a\), then the corresponding perturbation to the estimated trip matrix is

\[ \delta \log T_{ij} = \delta \log \tilde T_{ij}^0 - \delta m_{ij}^T \cdot \theta - m_{ij}^T \delta \theta \]

One last idea: how does uncertainty propagate?

The perturbation analysis gives us the perturbation of the transport matrix \(\frac{dT}{dV}\), as well as the perturbation of the OD effective distance \(\frac{\partial d_{ij}}{\partial d_b} = p_{ij}^b\) .

From this, it is possible to model

  • the variance in est. of T, based on characteristics of noise on edge measurements
  • the uncertainty in arrival time, based on characteristics of noise on edge measurements, since \[ t_{arrival}(i, j) \propto d_{ij}^{eff} \]
